{# The sticky chrome's real height, published as `--dash-nav-h` (UX W1, navigation-and-ia-17). `scroll-margin-top` on every anchor target has to equal the height of the rows pinned above it, and that height is one row on a desktop and three on a phone — so it is measured, not guessed. Without it a `#turn-N` or `#{chunk_id}` deep link lands with its heading under the sticky rows. One file, included by `dashboard/_base.html` and by `policy.html`. The reader carried a hard-coded `scroll-margin-top: 5.5rem` instead and landed its citations behind a 165px masthead at 390px, which is the same defect the dashboard had already measured its way out of (UX W6, nav-reaudit-2). The element measured is `#app-chrome`; a page without one simply publishes nothing and the CSS fallback in `.anchor, .turn-card, .chunk, [id^="turn-"]` applies. #}
