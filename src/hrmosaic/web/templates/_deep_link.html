{# Where a deep link lands (UX W5, accessibility-and-responsive-10). Two routes carry fragment links a reader arrives on from somewhere else: the dashboard's `/dashboard/sessions/{id}#turn-{seq}` (from the chat panel and from the turns listing) and the reader's `/policy/{doc_id}#{chunk_id}` (from every citation). Both scrolled and neither moved focus, so a keyboard or screen-reader user arrived at the top of the page and had to find the thing they had just followed a link to. `scroll-margin-top` — measured into `--dash-nav-h` by the page that has a sticky chrome — has already put the target in the right place, which is why the focus call is `preventScroll`. One file, included by both surfaces, so the two cannot drift. #}
