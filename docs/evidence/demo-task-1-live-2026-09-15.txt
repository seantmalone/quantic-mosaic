You have completed 45 months of continuous service as of 1 September 2026, which exceeds the 12-month minimum required to work outside your home country.

Your 42-day stay in Germany (3 November to 14 December) exceeds the 30-day threshold and therefore requires director approval, Tax & Legal review, and written notice at least 21 calendar days before departure.

Germany is an approved destination for temporary work abroad.

Your 42-day stay falls within the rolling 90-day annual limit for work outside your home country.

You must use a company-managed, full-disk-encrypted laptop with always-on VPN, and IT Security must be notified of your destination at least 5 business days before departure.

You may not sign contracts or negotiate customer agreements on behalf of Mosaic Robotics while abroad, and Tax & Legal review will typically approve stays over 30 days with conditions including a hard end date and prohibition on customer-facing activity.

International business travel of 30 consecutive days or fewer does not trigger Tax & Legal review if the activity is genuinely travel rather than ordinary conduct of your role from a foreign desk; however, your 42-day stay is work from another country, not business travel, and requires the full approval process.

Recommendation — not company policy: Raise your request in MosaicOne under 'Work from another country' with exact dates (3 November to 14 December 2026) and your workspace address, and file a Tax & Legal review request at least 21 calendar days before departure.
