## 4. Confirmed residuals

**4 Critical + 14 Important + 24 Minor**, de-duplicated across the seven lenses (one defect was filed
up to five times — e.g. the field-name sentence as `JX3-01`, `CPUX3-02`, `npo4-04`, `dgc-r3-7` and
`DR3-12`). Every one was re-checked against this capture; the load-bearing geometry and string claims

### Critical (4)

**C1 · The newest message is never on screen on a phone** — `CPUX3-01` = `a11y-r3-1`, carried from
`cpux2-1` = `a11y-re2-2` · P11, P12 · plan `chat-production-ux-2`
Screens: `chat-answer`, `chat-after-confirm`, `chat-refusal`, `chat-clarify`, `chat-error-degraded`,
`chat-confirm-card`, `chat-reloaded`, `chat-answer-dark` @390×844.
*Problem.* W7's `scroller()` fix is correct — below 30rem the document is the scroller — but
`stickToBottom()` writes `box.scrollTop = box.scrollHeight`, and under Addendum 2 the document's end is
the end of the always-expanded grader panel (~640–830px below the conversation). After every turn the
reader lands on the demo controls with no conversation visible; the composer's textarea is under the
119px sticky chrome (only Send is hittable); `#jump-latest` is hidden because `atBottom()` is true, and
when the reader scrolls back to the readable answer the button appears and, pressed, takes them off it
again.
*Evidence.* Live Playwright probe (local, `LLM_PROVIDER=stub`, no live model call) at 390×844 after
one answered turn: `scrollY 549 = docH 1393 − innerH 844`; last `.turn` top −418 / bottom −25;
`form.top −9`; `document.elementFromPoint(textarea centre)` → `masthead`; `#jump-latest.hidden` true.
Identical on a fresh `/?session=<id>` load and after a 1440→390 resize. Pixel proof:
`shots/chat-answer__390x844.fold.png`. Source: `chat.html:212-236`, `app.css:423-428`.
*Fix.* Stop treating "the bottom of the scroller" as "the newest message": scroll the newest turn's end
to just above the sticky composer (`lastTurn.scrollIntoView({block:'end'})` plus a composer offset, or
`conversation.offsetTop + conversation.offsetHeight − innerHeight`), and compute `atBottom()` from the
last turn rather than `document.scrollingElement.scrollHeight` so `#jump-latest` tracks the turn.
*Guard (the present one passes vacuously).* Replace `newest.bottom <= viewport`
(`tests/ux/test_principles.py:611`) with `0 < newest.bottom <= viewport` **and** `newest.top < viewport`,
plus `elementFromPoint` at the textarea's centre is `#message` — at 390×844 after a turn, after a
`/?session=` load, and after the 1440→390 resize leg.

**C2 · Chat prints a stored field name as English** — `JX3-01` = `CPUX3-02` = `npo4-04` = `dgc-r3-7` =
`DR3-12` · P2, P13, P9 · NEW, W8 C03
Screens: `chat-after-confirm`, `chat-after-confirm-expanded`, `zoom-turn-after-confirm` (×3 viewports)
and their dashboard twins — 15 dumps, 9 of them chat-surface.
*Problem.* The `record` block W7/W8 built so the reader's own data would read as fact ends every finished
PTO turn with *"Your notice business days is 0, and the policy asks for at least 5."* — the stored
`computed.notice_business_days` with its underscores swapped for spaces, ungrammatical, and the only
machine vocabulary left on the chat surface.
*Evidence.* `chat-after-confirm__1440x900.txt:31` (identical at 1280×800 and 390×844); pixel-confirmed
in `zoom-turn-after-confirm__1440x900.zoom.png` under the bold "From your HR record". Root cause verified
read-only: `src/hrmosaic/agent/compliance.py:246` `reader_sentence()` formats
`"Your {_label(subject)} is {value}, and the policy asks for {relation} {expected}."`, and `_label()`
(line 242) falls back to `leaf.replace("_", " ")`. `SUBJECT_LABELS` (line 131) covers 5 leaves;
`corpus/rules.yml` declares **25** (measured), so **20** reach the reader raw — and the boolean ones will
render "is yes/no". The module docstring uses this very sentence as its worked example, so the path is
intended, not accidental.
*Fix.* Give every `rules.yml` subject an entry in `SUBJECT_LABELS` phrased for a reader
("You gave 0 business days' notice, and the policy asks for at least 5"), and make the fallback refuse to
guess — fall back to the requirement-named form `reader_sentence` already has, never to
`leaf.replace("_", " ")`. Guards: a contract test that every subject in `corpus/rules.yml` resolves to a
label, and the de-underscored spelling of every subject added to the chat denylist (today's
`[a-z]+_[a-z]+` pattern cannot see this class at all).

**C3 · A performed write stands beside the rule it breaks, unreconciled** — `JX3-02`, with the capture
half as `dgc-r3-6` · P9, P13 · NEW, W8 C02/C04
Screens: `chat-after-confirm` (×3), `zoom-turn-after-confirm` (×3), `chat-confirm-card`,
`dashboard-session-demo2`, `dashboard-tools`.
*Problem.* In one turn the product (a) opens the ticket — *"Done — your request is with the HR Time Off
team. Reference MOCK-HR-000001. Your manager Dana's written approval is the next step."*, (b) quotes
*"PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance."*, (c) says the reader's notice is
0 — and then says nothing about the gap. This is worse than the pre-W8 state, where the same turn
asserted the requirement was **met**: the numbers are now honest and the narrative around them is not.
Neither dashboard surface carries the unmet row: the span summary is the bare
`check_policy_compliance · ok` while the envelope says `verdict: conditional`.
*Evidence.* `chat-after-confirm__1440x900.txt:15,17,31`; `dashboard-tools__1440x900.txt:43,59,63`
(`status: created · ticket id: MOCK-HR-000001` and `verdict: conditional · as of: 1 September 2026 ·
submitted on: 15…`); `dashboard-safety__1440x900.txt:66-72` (Confirmations "confirmed", Simulated writes
`MOCK-HR-000001`); `dashboard-session-demo2__1440x900.txt:155`.
*Two causes, two fixes.*
1. **Pipeline (certain).** `scripts/ux_capture.py` builds each stub server's env (lines ~185-195:
   `LLM_PROVIDER`, `LLM_STUB_SCRIPT`, `APP_ACCESS_TOKEN`, `APP_ENV`, `PERSIST_BACKEND`, `TRACE_DB_PATH`,
   `EMBED_WARMUP`, `PORT`) with **no `MOCK_TODAY`**, while `Makefile:18` pins `MOCK_TODAY ?= 2026-09-01`
   for `make demo1`/`demo2` (Makefile:90) and `tests/conftest.py:25` sets the same default. W8's C04
   states plainly: *"recorded stubs keep their fixed dates and run with MOCK_TODAY … so every replay
   scores the same."* The recorded stub still asks for 15–17 September 2026, so the engine anchors
   `submitted_on` on the wall clock (the capture ran on the request's own start date) and scores the
   notice at 0 — and the drift grows every day the capture is re-run. **Fix:** pass
   `"MOCK_TODAY": os.environ.get("MOCK_TODAY", "2026-09-01")` into the stub env and re-capture.
2. **Product (unproven either way by this capture, and that is itself the defect).** Nothing on these
   screens demonstrates C02's coupling ("a blocking non-compliance stops the card"), because the turn
   that reached a performed write with an unmet blocking row is exactly what C02 forbids. **Fix:** when a
   same-turn compliance row is `unmet` on the requirement a performed write is about, either the card is
   not proposed (C02) or the turn states the consequence in the reader's voice beside the `performed`
   block; surface the per-requirement status in the span summary so `ok` cannot stand for a conditional
   verdict with an unmet row. Guard: a contract test on the demo-2 stub that no turn may contain a
   performed write **and** an unmet blocking requirement with no sentence naming the shortfall; and a
   dedicated screen id that photographs C02's refusal branch deliberately rather than by accident.

## 8. Method

* **Screens.** `make ux-capture` against the working tree (`scripts/ux_capture.py`, four stub servers
  sharing one trace store, `LLM_PROVIDER=stub`, fresh page per viewport since W7's Addendum) →
  `scratchpad/uxreaudit3/` — 71 screen ids × 3 viewports, light and dark, each with `.full.png`,
  `.fold.png`, `.txt`, `.numbers.json`, `.overflow.json`. **No live LLM call.**
* **Mechanical rules.** Plan §1's detection rules re-run over all 213 dumps and 213 overflow sidecars
  (P1, P2, P7, P9), over the templates and Python view-models (P8, P10, P13, P15), and over a live crawl
  of 121 routes as the default persona (P4, P5, P6).
* **Live verification** (local Playwright venv at `scratchpad/pwvenv`, dedicated stub servers) wherever
  the capture could not see the state: phone first paint and the `/?session=` and resize legs, the
  in-flight status line, the deep-link landing geometry at three viewports, the citation round trip,
  duplicate accessible names at 390, nav and chrome heights on all ten dashboard routes, tab ARIA state.
* **Before/after** is always the same screen id at the same viewport in `scratchpad/uxreaudit2/shots/`;
  every geometry claim in §4 was re-measured from both captures' own `.overflow.json` sidecars rather
  than taken from the lens reports.
* **Re-check protocol.** Each of re-audit #2's confirmed findings (36 in `confirmed-findings.json`) was
  re-checked one by one: **22 verified fixed, 14 carried in some form** — and the 14 collapse to 11
  distinct defects, because the phone scroll regression was filed three times (`cpux2-1`, `cpux2-2`'s
  phone half, `a11y-re2-2`). Findings that contradict a written
  W7/W8/owner ruling are excluded from the residual count and listed in §5.
* **Repo read-only.** Nothing edited, staged or committed; `.env` never read; no subagents used.
