## 2. The owner's three goals

### (a) Production-grade chat — **NOT MET**

Almost everything the goal asks for is there. Chat is clean of internal identifiers (P2 clean,
0 hits), the copy is plain (P13 clean), sources are friendly references that expand in place,
the composer is sticky, the empty state's content is exactly the §3.1 wireframe (greeting,
one-line capability statement, four starter chips, the `/policy` link), and the phone leg is
correct at every screen.

It is not met because **the landing screen is visibly broken at both desktop viewports**. A first
visitor at 1440×900 sees starter 3 sliced through the middle of its box by the composer card, and
starter 4 and the "Or read the policies themselves" link simply absent — and the page does not
scroll, so there is nothing the reader can do to reveal them. The numbers are byte-identical to
re-audit #3 (288 / 425 in both captures), on a defect the W8 fix round reported closed as I12.
Once a turn exists the same 288px box is the whole conversation. Two further chat-surface defects
land on the flagship refusal demo the fix round itself added: a sentence printed twice in one turn,
and an uncited numeric policy threshold inside the block that is drawn without a citation by design
(CPUX4-02), plus a comparator that attributes the reader's own request to the policy (npo5-02).

Evidence: `chat-rest__{1440x900,1280x800}.overflow.json`, `chat-rest__1440x900.fold.png`,
`zoom-empty-state__1440x900.zoom.png`, `chat-write-blocked__1440x900.txt`.

### (b) Demo controls quarantined and labelled — **MET**

One `section.demo-panel`, dashed border, muted ground that is visible in dark mode,
`<h2>Demo & grader controls</h2>`, the disclaimer "For evaluation only — a real user never sees
this panel.", the 24-employee picker and both demo prompts inside it, the session-id chip, the
deep link, the produced line, the environment block and the demo sign-out. Nothing demo-shaped
exists outside it, at any viewport, in either theme; the contract test holds the boundary. The
panel is always expanded per the owner's 2026-09-15 ruling, and its share of the page is the
owner's decision, not a finding (CPUX4-BR1). The only caveat is that the ruling gives the panel
*the page's* scroll, and the capture shows the conversation paying with its height instead —
which is CPUX4-01/CPUX4-04 against goal (a), not against this one.

### (c) All technical detail in the dashboard, with a deep link — **NOT MET (met in structure, failed in legibility)**

The relocation is complete and the deep link works. Nothing technical remains in chat (P2: 0 hits
over every chat dump). The dashboard carries the full record with `Export this page as JSON` on
every page. `deeplink-session-turn1` lands the Turn 1 card wholly below the 107px chrome at 1440,
1280 and 390, tinted and outlined by `:target` — `scroll-margin-top` and the highlight both shipped.

It is not met because the one page every chat deep link and every turn-id chip lands on renders the
record unreadable: **the OPENED step payload is a 35px-wide ribbon** — one or two characters per
line — inside a 352px-tall, mouse-wheel-only box, at both desktop widths, on every session page.
The largest payload holds 372,235px of wrapped text, i.e. roughly 1,057 viewports of scrolling at
two characters a line, and a keyboard user cannot move it at all. Re-audit #3 already measured
`35 / 35` and scored only the page height, so this has now survived two fix rounds. Two lesser
gaps: the "Opened from chat" breadcrumb of `dashboard-readability-29` never shipped (DR4-10), and
the `dashboard-readability-21` methodology work shipped over numbers that are not honest yet
(DR4-02, DR4-03).

Evidence: `dashboard-session-demo1__1440x900.overflow.json` (28 entries, `scrollWidth 35 /
clientWidth 35`), `scratchpad/a11y4/wf_crop.png`, `deeplink-session-turn1__1440x900.fold.png`.

## 4. Confirmed residuals — 1 Critical, 13 Important

### Critical

**A11Y4-01 — the session waterfall's OPENED payload is a 35px ribbon at every desktop width**
P7 / P15. Screens: `dashboard-session-demo1@{1440x900,1280x800}`, `dashboard-session-demo2@1440x900`,
`dashboard-session-demo1-dark@1440x900`, `deeplink-session-turn1@{1440x900,1280x800}`,
`deeplink-403-employee@1440x900`.
*Problem.* The W8 fix round did what DR3-01 asked (`grid-row: 1` on row items, `grid-row: 2` on the
payload) and the page height came back to budget — `dashboard-session-demo1__1440x900.full.png` is
now 1440×**2,724** against re-audit #3's 4,372 — but the payload's `grid-column: 1 / -1` never takes
effect. Chrome wraps a `<details>` body in a `::details-content` box, so with
`.span-payload { display: contents }` the grid item is that anonymous wrapper, not the `<pre>` the
rule targets, and the wrapper is auto-placed into track 1 (35.19px). The unrounded technical record
that chat was emptied into (§2C, P15) is unreadable on the exact page every deep link lands on.
*Evidence.* `dashboard-session-demo1__1440x900.overflow.json` — 28 entries for
`article#turn-1 > ol.waterfall > li.span-row > details.span-payload > pre.mono`, every one
`scrollWidth 35 / clientWidth 35`, `scrollHeight` 2,574–372,235 against `clientHeight 352`; identical
in `deeplink-session-turn1__1280x800.overflow.json`. Live confirmation at 1440×900 (LOCAL, stub, no
live model call): `li` `gridTemplateColumns 35.1875px 120px 224px 601.438px 224px 80px 25.5938px`,
`pre` computed `grid-column: 1/-1` but used rect `{width: 35.19, height: 352}`. Pixel proof
`scratchpad/a11y4/wf_crop.png` — the payload reads vertically `{ / "k / in / d" / : / "g / ua / rd`.
Source: app.css:1109, :1117, :1095; `templates/dashboard/session_detail.html:150-152`. History:
original audit `4448 / 1358`; re-audit 2 `1358 / 1358`; re-audit 3 already `35 / 35` — two fix
rounds have passed over it because the budget measures height only.
*Fix.* Stop relying on `display: contents` on `<details>`: (a) render `<summary>` as the row's 7th-track
control and the `<pre>` as a full-width block in a following row wrapper that is itself the grid item
spanning `1 / -1`; or (b) add `.span-payload::details-content { display: contents; grid-column: 1 / -1;
grid-row: 2 }` with a non-grid fallback; or (c) place a wrapper and give the `<pre>` `width: 100%`
inside it. **Guard:** at 1440 and 1280 with every `details.span-payload` open, assert each payload
`<pre>`'s `clientWidth ≥ 60%` of its `li.span-row` width — the present height-only budget (≤3,400px
at 1440) passes while the box is 35px wide.

## 8. Verdict

**FAIL. Another fix round is required before this can be called done.**

The trajectory is good and most of the plan has landed: 9 of 15 principles pass outright, 89 of the
96 inventory ids under the four lenses re-audited are fixed, three of re-audit #3's four Criticals
are genuinely closed, the demo quarantine is complete, chat is clean of every internal identifier
and every piece of infrastructure vocabulary, no document scrolls sideways at any of 390 screens,
and the technical record is fully relocated with an unrounded JSON export on every page.

It fails on three grounds, in order of weight:

1. **One Critical is open and has now survived two fix rounds** (**A11Y4-01**). The record the whole
   dashboard exists to carry is a 35px ribbon at both desktop widths, on the page every chat deep
   link lands on, and a keyboard user cannot scroll it at all. It survived because the guard written
   for it measures only page height — the fix round's own budget passes while the box is 35px wide.
2. **A defect reported as fixed is not fixed, with byte-identical numbers** (**CPUX4-01**). The W8
   fix round recorded I12 as "empty state fully painted at rest"; the capture shows 288 / 425 at
   1440×900 and 1280×800, exactly as in re-audit #3, and at 1440 the page does not scroll at all.
   The shipped CSS cannot have had any effect at rest. This is the product's landing screen.
3. **The honesty work regressed into confident wrong numbers.** P14's rule was satisfied by printing
   denominators; three of them are the wrong denominator (**DR4-02**, **DR4-03**, **npo5-01**) and
   one number is a zero that is not a measurement (**npo5-04**). A denominator in the cell is harder
   for a grader to discount than the footnote it replaced.

**Residuals: 14 serious (1 Critical, 13 Important) and 22 Minor.** Six of the serious ones are
single-line template or CSS edits (CPUX4-01, DR4-01, DR4-04, plus the guards); four require the
evaluation runner to publish a figure it does not publish today (DR4-02, DR4-03, npo5-01, npo5-04).

**The one lesson for the next round's guards:** every regression that survived this round survived a
guard that measured the wrong axis — height where the defect was width (A11Y4-01), containment where
the defect was `min-height` (CPUX4-01), presence of a denominator where the defect was its value
(DR4-02/03), viewport width where the defect was available space (A11Y4-03). Each fix below carries
a guard written against the *measured* property, and each should be asserted to **fail on the current
build** before the change lands.
