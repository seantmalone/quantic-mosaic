### (a) Production-grade chat — **NOT MET**
Everything re-audit #1 credited still holds, and three of its four chat Criticals are gone:

* The completed write is now the turn's lead sentence, outside the suggestions group and outside its

  (nav-reaudit-1 closed.)
* No employee id, no ISO date, no internal identifier anywhere in chat (P2 clean).

It is not production-grade because of four things a real user meets before a grader would:

1. **On a phone, the conversation does not move.** Ask a question, and the viewport stays at the top
   of your own message with the answer 955px below the fold and no "Jump to latest" (**R0**, Critical).
2. **The reader's own HR facts are filed as advice.** *"You have 13.5 PTO days remaining… Your request
   … meets the 5-business-day notice requirement (8 business days' notice)"* prints under **"What I
   suggest you do"** and under *"Suggestions are guidance, not company policy."* Those two sentences
   are `check_pto_balance` and `check_policy_compliance` results — telling the reader their own
   balance is non-binding (**R12**, verified live on the current tree).
3. **The screen-reader script is painted as visible UI.** Every finished turn ends with a grey
   bulleted line — `● Answer ready.`, `● Could you clarify?` — and on a refusal `● I can't answer that
   one — see below.` points at empty space, the composer and the demo panel (**R20**).
4. **Nothing is re-measured on a viewport change**, so a rotated phone or a resized window keeps the
   composer height and scroll position computed for the other width (**R3**).

### (b) Demo controls quarantined and labelled — **MET**
`section.demo-panel` remains the sole home of `#actor-select`, `.demo-button` and the grader deep
link; its `<summary>` carries the `<h2>Demo & grader controls</h2>` and the disclaimer. Both caveats

### (c) All technical detail in the dashboard, with a deep link — **MET**
The relocation holds and the round trip is verified in both directions. Every chart is now legible —
the Critical of re-audit #1 is fixed: canvases measure **1361px in a 1400px panel (ratio 0.97)** on
Overview and Guardrails, where they were ~300px. `Export this page as JSON` is on every page with

### Critical (1)

**R0 · The newest turn is never brought into view on a phone, and "Jump to latest" is dead.**
*(P11 / `chat-production-ux-2`; regression, W6; lenses `cpux2-1` = `a11y-re2-2`)*
W6 correctly stopped `#transcript` being a viewport-sized scroll box below 30rem (the owner's "a phone
is a document" ruling). Every scroll mechanism in `chat.html` still addresses `transcript.scrollTop`:
`stickToBottom()` writes to an element whose computed `overflow-y` is now `visible`, so it is a no-op,
and `atBottom()` — which gates `#jump-latest` — returns true forever. Nothing scrolls the page.
**Evidence (live, fresh 390×844 load, own stub server, no resize):** after turn 1 `scrollY 0`,
`docH 2167`, `vh 844`, last turn bottom **1799** (955px below the fold), `#jump-latest.hidden true`,
`transcript.overflowY "visible"`, `scrollHeight == clientHeight == 1725`; after turn 2 `scrollY 0`,
`docH 2504`, new turn top 1823 with the composer pinned at 657. `verify/probe1.json`,
`verify/fresh390_turn2.png`. Before W6 the same state stuck correctly
(`uxreaudit/shots/chat-answer__390x844.overflow.json`: `div#transcript` 366/366 inside a 369px box).
**Fix.** Resolve the scroller at runtime —
`var scroller = getComputedStyle(transcript).overflowY === 'visible' ? document.scrollingElement : transcript;`
— and use it in `stickToBottom()`, `atBottom()` and the scroll listener (bind to `window` when the
scroller is the document). Re-resolve on `resize`/`matchMedia('(max-width: 30rem)')`. Add a 390×844 ux
assertion that after a second turn the last `.turn` is in the viewport **or** `#jump-latest` is visible.

## 7. Method

* **Screens.** `make ux-capture` against the working tree (`scripts/ux_capture.py`, four stub servers
  sharing one trace store, `LLM_PROVIDER=stub`) → `scratchpad/uxreaudit2/` — 69 screen ids × 3
  viewports, light and dark, `.full.png` / `.fold.png` / `.txt` / `.numbers.json` / `.overflow.json`.
* **Mechanical rules.** Plan §1's detection rules re-run over all 207 dumps and 207 overflow files
  (P1, P2, P7, P9), over the templates (P8, P10, P13, P15) and over a live crawl (P4, P5).
* **Live verification.** Four Playwright probes (`scratchpad/uxreaudit2/verify/`) against dedicated
  stub servers, used wherever the capture could not see the state: fresh-load-per-viewport phone
  measurements, the in-flight status line, the citation → reader → back round trip, the confirm path
  on the current working tree, chart geometry, tab/ARIA state, `aria-current` scroll position,
  duplicate accessible names at 390, and `curl` of a chip's destination content-type.
* **Working-tree caveat.** The capture was taken at 11:24, on `7655321`. While this audit ran, two
  further commits landed (`48c1c8d` P29(outcome) — the `performed` statement is the one account of a
  write whatever the model wrote; `d8a2ca3` P29(snapshot) — a restated snapshot date and a tenure in
  months are corrected). Both are agent-side; the web layer (`templates/`, `app.css`, `api.py`) is
  unchanged since 10:11, so every layout finding holds for the current tree, and the two
  answer-content findings (R12, and the performed write) were re-verified **live against the tree
  carrying those edits** — R12 reproduces, the performed write is fixed.
* **No live LLM call was made; the deployed URL was never touched; `.env` was never read; the repo was
  not modified.**
