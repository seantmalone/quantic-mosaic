# Leave of Absence Policy

Document ID: leave-of-absence · Owner: People Operations · Effective 2026-01-01 · Version 2026.1
Topics: leave

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes the extended absences Mosaic Robotics supports: parental leave, medical leave, family care leave, bereavement, civic duty and
unpaid personal leave. It applies to employees of Mosaic Robotics, Inc. and, with the differences noted in the *Germany Entity Differences* section, to
employees of Mosaic Robotics GmbH.

A leave of absence is different from PTO in three ways that matter. It is longer, it is protected — the employee's job and benefits continue on defined
terms — and it is administered by People Operations rather than by the employee's manager. Nothing in this policy consumes PTO, and an employee does
not need to exhaust PTO before taking a leave.

Where a statutory entitlement exists that is more generous than the company benefit described here, the statutory entitlement applies and the company
benefit is layered on top rather than substituted for it. People Operations administers the interaction; the employee does not need to work it out.

Every leave is recorded in MosaicOne with a start date, an expected return date, the type of leave and the pay treatment. Managers see the dates and the
type. Managers do not see medical information, and People Operations does not share it with them.

## Types of Leave

### Parental Leave

Mosaic Robotics provides 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to every eligible new parent. The 16 weeks are the same whether the employee gave birth,
did not give birth, adopted, or became a parent through surrogacy or foster placement leading to adoption.

Parental leave eligibility begins after 6 months of continuous service. An employee who becomes a parent before reaching six months receives the
statutory entitlement their jurisdiction provides, and the company benefit begins the day the service requirement is met.

Leave must begin within 12 months of the birth or placement. It may be taken as one continuous block, or as up to three blocks of at least two weeks
each, agreed with the manager and People Operations before the first block begins. Splitting the leave does not extend the total.

An employee who gives birth also receives any period of medical disability leave certified by their clinician, before the parental leave begins. The
disability period is paid through short-term disability rather than as parental leave, and it does not reduce the 16 weeks.

Parental leave for both parents where both work at Mosaic Robotics is 16 weeks each. The company does not pool or reduce the entitlement.

### Medical Leave

Continuous medical leave is available for up to 26 weeks in a rolling 12-month period. The leave covers the employee's own serious health condition,
including physical illness, surgery, recovery and mental health treatment.

The first 13 weeks are paid at 70% of base salary through short-term disability after a 7-day elimination period covered by the employee's sick balance
or PTO. Weeks 14 to 26 are paid at 60% of base salary through long-term disability. Mosaic Robotics tops the first two weeks up to 100% of base salary
so that a short recovery is not a financial event.

Intermittent medical leave — a course of treatment requiring a day a week, a reduced schedule during recovery — is available on the same 26-week
budget, counted in hours rather than weeks. The schedule is agreed with People Operations and the manager is told only the schedule.

A clinician's certification is required for any medical leave longer than five consecutive working days, and a recertification is required every eight
weeks for a continuing leave. Certifications go to People Operations and are stored in a restricted-access record separate from the employee file.

### Family Care Leave

Family care leave provides up to 12 weeks in a rolling 12-month period to care for a spouse, registered domestic partner, child, parent, parent-in-law
or sibling with a serious health condition, or for a family member's military deployment.

The first 4 weeks are paid at 100% of base salary. The remaining 8 weeks are unpaid, and the employee may use PTO to cover part or all of them if they
choose. Benefits continue in full for the whole 12 weeks.

Family care leave may be taken continuously or intermittently, and the 12 weeks are separate from the 26 weeks of medical leave. An employee who needs
both in the same year receives both.

### Bereavement Leave

Bereavement leave provides 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family means a spouse or partner, a child, a parent, a
sibling, a grandparent, a grandchild, and the equivalent in-law relationships.

Three paid days are provided for the death of any other person with whom the employee had a close relationship, at the employee's own description; the
company does not adjudicate the relationship.

Bereavement leave is taken within 60 days of the death and need not be consecutive, so that an employee can attend a service that happens weeks later.
Additional time is available as PTO or as unpaid personal leave, and People Operations will arrange it without requiring the employee to negotiate.

Pregnancy loss is bereavement leave, at 5 paid days, for both parents.

### Jury Duty and Civic Leave

Jury service is paid at full salary for up to 10 days per plan year. Longer service is paid at full salary where the employee assigns the court's
stipend to the company, and People Operations arranges this with payroll.

Time spent responding to a subpoena in a matter connected to the employee's Mosaic Robotics work is paid working time and is not counted against civic
leave.

Voting leave is provided as the law of the employee's jurisdiction requires, and in every case Mosaic Robotics provides at least two paid hours to vote
where the polls are not open outside the employee's working hours.

Volunteer emergency service — a volunteer firefighter, a search-and-rescue team member, a reserve responder — is supported with up to 5 paid days per
plan year for call-outs and required training.

### Unpaid Personal Leave

Unpaid personal leave of up to 12 weeks may be requested for a reason not covered elsewhere in this policy: study, an extended family commitment, a
personal project. It is granted at the discretion of the department head with People Operations' agreement, and it is not an entitlement.

Benefits continue during unpaid personal leave, with the employee paying their ordinary share of the premium by direct payment to payroll. PTO does not
accrue during unpaid leave and the connectivity stipend is not paid.

An employee on unpaid personal leave keeps their role for the agreed period. A leave longer than 12 weeks is possible only where the department head
and the VP of People Operations agree in advance that the role can be held open.

## Requesting a Leave

Foreseeable leave must be requested at least 30 calendar days in advance. The request is raised in MosaicOne under *Request a leave of absence* and
names the type of leave, the intended start date and the expected duration.

Unforeseeable leave — an accident, a sudden diagnosis, a death — is requested as soon as the employee reasonably can, by any means, to their manager or
directly to People Operations. Nothing in this policy requires a sick person to file a form before they receive care.

People Operations responds to every leave request within 3 business days with a written summary: the type of leave approved, the pay treatment, the
benefits treatment, what certification is needed and by when, and who the employee's named contact will be for the duration.

The manager's role is to plan coverage, not to approve the leave. A statutory or company leave entitlement is not a request a manager can refuse, and
People Operations will tell a manager so plainly if it comes up.

An employee may extend a leave by contacting People Operations before the expected return date. An extension within the entitlement is administrative;
an extension beyond it is considered as unpaid personal leave.

## Benefits During Leave

Medical, dental, vision, life and disability cover continue unchanged during every paid leave in this policy, with the employee's share of the premium
deducted from any pay they receive and invoiced where pay is insufficient.

During unpaid leave the employee remains enrolled and pays their ordinary share by direct payment to payroll. Missing three consecutive payments ends
cover, and People Operations gives 30 days of written warning before that happens.

PTO accrues normally during paid leave. It does not accrue during unpaid leave, and it does not accrue during any period in which the employee is
receiving long-term disability benefit.

401(k) contributions continue from any pay the employee receives, and the company match continues on the same terms. Contributions stop during unpaid
periods and resume automatically on return, at the same rate.

Service for the purpose of tenure, PTO accrual bands and vesting continues to accumulate during every protected leave, paid or unpaid.

## Return to Work

An employee returning from leave returns to the same role, at the same level and the same base pay. Where the role no longer exists because of an
organisational change that would have affected the employee regardless of the leave, they return to an equivalent role, and People Operations reviews
the change before it is made.

A return from medical leave requires a clinician's fitness-to-return statement, which says only whether the employee is fit and what accommodations if
any are needed. It contains no diagnosis and is held by People Operations.

Where a phased return is recommended — reduced hours for a period, a temporary change of duties — People Operations arranges it with the manager. A
phased return is normal after a long absence and is not a performance signal.

An employee who cannot return at the end of their entitlement is supported through a reasonable-accommodation review before any other conversation
happens. Termination at the end of a leave is a last resort, requires VP of People Operations approval, and never happens without that review.

## Germany Entity Differences

German employees receive statutory Mutterschutz, Elternzeit and Pflegezeit entitlements, which are more extensive than the company parental and family
care benefits described above. The company benefit tops up statutory pay to 100% of net salary for the first 16 weeks of Elternzeit.

Medical absence in Germany is covered by statutory Entgeltfortzahlung for the first six weeks at full pay, followed by Krankengeld from the statutory
health insurer. The company's short-term disability plan does not apply; the company tops up Krankengeld to 90% of net salary for up to 26 weeks.

German statutory vacation entitlement interacts with the PTO carryover rules in the PTO & Holidays Policy. Where the statute requires a longer carryover
window, the statute applies.

All other sections of this policy — request mechanics, benefits continuation, return to work, confidentiality — apply to German employees unchanged.

## Sabbaticals

Mosaic Robotics offers a paid sabbatical of 4 consecutive weeks to every employee who reaches 5 years of
continuous service, and again at each subsequent five-year anniversary. The sabbatical is in addition to
PTO and does not draw down any balance.

Eligibility is checked automatically in MosaicOne on the service anniversary, and the employee is
notified. The sabbatical must be taken within 24 months of the anniversary or it lapses; it cannot be
banked, deferred indefinitely, or exchanged for pay.

A sabbatical is requested at least 90 calendar days in advance so that coverage can be planned properly.
The manager and the department head agree the dates; the entitlement itself is not discretionary and is
never refused, only scheduled.

The point of a sabbatical is a genuine break, so an employee on sabbatical is removed from on-call
rotations, from meeting invitations and from routine distribution lists, and their accounts stay active
only so that nothing breaks on their return. Colleagues are asked not to contact them about work, and
managers are accountable for that.

Benefits, PTO accrual, service accumulation and 401(k) contributions all continue unchanged during a
sabbatical, which is paid at 100% of base salary.

An employee may combine a sabbatical with PTO to make a longer break, subject to the ordinary notice
rules for the PTO portion. An employee may not combine a sabbatical with a leave of absence; where the
two would overlap, the leave takes precedence and the sabbatical is rescheduled.

## Contacts

Leave requests and all questions about entitlement, pay treatment or certification go to People Operations at leave@mosaicrobotics.example. Urgent
matters outside business hours go to people-ops@mosaicrobotics.example, which is monitored daily. Disability claims are handled by the carrier, with
People Operations as the employee's advocate where a claim is delayed or refused.
