Benefits and Open Enrollment Guide

Document ID: benefits-and-open-enrollment · Owner: People Operations · Effective 2026-01-01 · Version 2026.1

Topics: benefits

Purpose and Scope

This guide describes the health, retirement and protection benefits Mosaic Robotics offers, who is eligible for them, when elections can be made, and how to change an election when life changes. It is written for employees of Mosaic Robotics, Inc. in the United States. Employees of Mosaic Robotics GmbH are covered by the German statutory system supplemented by the company plans described in the Germany Entity Supplement section.

Benefits are administered in MosaicOne Benefits, which is the only place an election is valid. A conversation with a manager, a message to People Operations or a form sent to the carrier does not change an election; the election in MosaicOne does.

This guide is a summary written in plain language. The legal terms of every plan are in that plan's summary plan description, which is linked from MosaicOne Benefits. Where this guide and a summary plan description disagree, the summary plan description governs, and People Operations will explain the difference rather than defend the summary.

Nothing here is a promise of future benefits. Mosaic Robotics reviews its benefit offering each year and may change carriers, tiers or contribution levels for a future plan year, with at least 60 days of notice before the change takes effect.

Eligibility

Waiting Period

New hires become eligible for medical, dental and vision coverage 90 days after their hire date. Coverage begins on the day the waiting period ends, not on the first of the following month, so an employee whose waiting period ends on 13 November is covered from 13 November.

An employee has 30 days from the end of the waiting period to make elections in MosaicOne Benefits. An employee who makes no election within that window is enrolled in employee-only coverage on the standard medical plan by default, and may not change it until the next open enrollment or a qualifying life event.

Retirement plan eligibility is different and shorter: an employee may begin contributing to the 401(k) plan from their first payroll run, with no waiting period at all.

The waiting period is not restarted by an internal transfer, a promotion, or a change from part-time to full-time status. It is restarted by a break in service of more than 26 weeks.

Employees scheduled at 0.5 FTE or more are eligible for the full benefit programme. Employees below 0.5 FTE, contractors and interns are not eligible for company medical, dental, vision or life cover, and are eligible for the 401(k) plan only where the plan document requires it.

Dependent Eligibility

Dependent children may remain on a Mosaic Robotics medical plan until the end of the month in which they turn 26. Dental and vision plans follow the same rule.

Eligible dependents are a legal spouse, a registered domestic partner, a biological or adopted child, a stepchild, and a child for whom the employee has legal guardianship. Documentation is required within 60 days of adding a dependent; a dependent whose documentation is not supplied is removed from coverage retroactively to the date they were added.

A child of any age who is incapable of self-support because of a disability that began before age 26 may remain covered indefinitely, subject to the carrier's certification.

Plan Year

The benefits plan year runs from 1 January 2026 through 31 December 2026. Every annual maximum, deductible, contribution limit and allowance in this guide resets on 1 January.

Elections made during open enrollment take effect on the first day of the following plan year. Elections made after a qualifying life event take effect on the date of the event, or on the first of the following month where the carrier requires it.

Deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums do not carry over between plan years, and neither do FSA balances beyond the carryover the plan permits. Employees who change plans mid-year through a qualifying life event should expect their deductible progress to reset with the new plan.

Open Enrollment

The 2026 Enrollment Window

Open enrollment opens on 1 November 2026. Open enrollment closes on 21 November 2026 at 23:59 US Central Time.

The window is three weeks and it does not move. MosaicOne Benefits sends reminders at the opening, at the halfway point and on the final two days, and People Operations runs two live question sessions in the second week, one at a time that works for Berlin.

An employee who makes no election during open enrollment keeps their current medical, dental and vision elections into the following plan year, at the following year's contribution rates. Flexible spending account elections are the exception: an FSA election never rolls over and must be made again every year, or the employee has no FSA.

Elections may be changed as often as the employee likes while the window is open. The election recorded when the window closes is the one that takes effect.

What You Can Change

During open enrollment an employee may change medical, dental and vision plans and tiers, add or remove dependents, elect or decline life and disability cover above the company-paid baseline, and set health and dependent-care flexible spending account contributions for the coming year.

401(k) contribution rates, investment elections and beneficiary designations are not tied to open enrollment and may be changed at any time in MosaicOne Benefits, taking effect on the next payroll run.

Employees adding a dependent during open enrollment must supply documentation before the window closes. A dependent added without documentation is not enrolled, and the employee finds out in January rather than in November, which is why the reminders are insistent.

Medical Plans

Mosaic Robotics offers three medical plans for 2026: the Standard PPO, the Core HDHP with a health savings account, and the Regional HMO available in Texas and Massachusetts only.

The Standard PPO carries an in-network individual deductible of USD 1,000 and an individual out-of-pocket maximum of USD 4,500. The Core HDHP carries an in-network individual deductible of USD 3,300 and an individual out-of-pocket maximum of USD 6,000. The Regional HMO has no deductible for in-network care and an individual out-of-pocket maximum of USD 3,500.

Mosaic Robotics pays 85% of the employee-only premium and 70% of the premium for each additional covered tier across all three plans. The remaining premium is deducted from pay on a pre-tax basis over 24 semi-monthly payroll runs.

Telemedicine visits are covered at no cost on all three plans. Mental health visits are covered on the same terms as any other specialist visit, with no separate visit limit, on all three plans.

Prescription coverage is included with every medical plan. Generic drugs carry a USD 10 copay on the PPO and the HMO, and are subject to the deductible on the HDHP.

Dental and Vision

The dental plan covers preventive care at 100% with no deductible, basic restorative care at 80% and major restorative care at 50%, with an annual maximum benefit of USD 2,000 per covered person. Orthodontia carries a separate lifetime maximum of USD 2,500 and is available to covered dependents under 19.

The vision plan covers one eye examination per plan year at a USD 10 copay, and provides an allowance of USD 200 per plan year toward frames or contact lenses. Employees whose role requires safety eyewear receive prescription safety glasses through Facilities rather than through the vision plan, at no cost and outside the allowance.

Dental and vision elections are independent of the medical election. An employee may decline medical cover and still elect dental and vision, or the reverse.

Retirement

Mosaic Robotics matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible compensation contributed to the 401(k) plan. An employee contributing 4% or more therefore receives the full match; an employee contributing 2% receives a 2% match.

Employer matching contributions vest after 2 years of service. Employee contributions are always immediately and fully vested, because they are the employee's own money.

Both traditional pre-tax and Roth after-tax contributions are available, and an employee may split between them. Contribution rate changes take effect on the next payroll run and may be made as often as the employee wishes.

New hires are automatically enrolled at a 4% pre-tax contribution rate 30 days after their start date unless they opt out or choose a different rate. The automatic rate increases by one percentage point each January until it reaches 10%; an employee may switch the escalation off at any time.

Loans and hardship withdrawals are available under the terms of the plan document. Rollovers into the plan from a previous employer are accepted at any time.

HSA and FSA

Mosaic Robotics contributes USD 750 per plan year to the HSA of each employee enrolled in the high-deductible medical plan. The company contribution is paid in two instalments, in January and July, and belongs to the employee immediately.

Employees enrolled in the HDHP may make their own pre-tax HSA contributions up to the annual statutory limit, inclusive of the company contribution. An HSA balance is the employee's property, carries over indefinitely and travels with them if they leave.

A health flexible spending account is available to employees on the PPO and HMO plans, up to the annual statutory limit. FSA balances do not carry over indefinitely: the plan permits a carryover of up to USD 640 into the following plan year, and anything above that is forfeited.

A dependent-care flexible spending account is available to every eligible employee regardless of medical election, up to the annual statutory limit. Dependent-care FSA balances carry over nothing at all; the funds must be used for expenses incurred within the plan year.

Life and Disability

Mosaic Robotics provides basic life insurance and accidental death and dismemberment cover at two times base salary, up to a maximum of USD 500,000, at no cost to the employee. Cover begins when the medical waiting period ends.

Employees may buy supplemental life cover for themselves up to a further five times base salary, and spouse and child cover at fixed amounts. Supplemental cover elected within 30 days of first eligibility is issued without medical underwriting; elected later, it requires evidence of insurability.

Short-term disability pays 70% of base salary for up to 13 weeks after a 7-day elimination period, at no cost to the employee. Long-term disability pays 60% of base salary after 13 weeks, to a monthly maximum of USD 12,000, also at no cost.

Disability cover interacts with the Leave of Absence Policy. An employee on a medical leave receives disability benefit rather than salary, and People Operations coordinates the two so there is no gap in pay.

Qualifying Life Events

A qualifying life event must be reported within 30 days of the event to change elections outside open enrollment. The 30 days run from the date of the event, not from the date the employee learns the rules.

Qualifying life events are marriage, divorce or legal separation, the birth or adoption of a child, the death of a covered dependent, a change in the employee's or a spouse's employment that changes benefit eligibility, a dependent losing or gaining other coverage, a change in residence that changes plan availability, and a court order requiring coverage of a child.

The change made must be consistent with the event. Marriage permits adding a spouse and moving to a family tier; it does not permit switching from the HMO to the PPO for unrelated reasons.

Documentation of the event is required within 60 days. A change made without documentation is reversed, and the employee returns to their prior elections retroactively.

Germany Entity Supplement

Employees of Mosaic Robotics GmbH participate in the German statutory health, pension, unemployment and long-term care systems, with contributions split between employer and employee as the statute requires. The plans described above do not apply.

The company supplements the statutory position with a private supplementary health plan, an occupational pension contribution of 5% of gross salary into a company-supported scheme, and a monthly public transport allowance. There is no waiting period for the supplementary plans; they begin on the first day of employment.

German employees do not participate in open enrollment. Their supplementary elections are made at hire and may be changed at any time with one month's notice.

Wellbeing and Family Support

Beyond the insured plans, Mosaic Robotics funds a small set of programmes aimed at the things people actually contact People Operations about.

The employee assistance programme provides eight counselling sessions per issue per plan year, at no cost, for the employee and every member of their household. It is confidential: the provider reports usage volume to the company and never reports who used it or why. The programme also covers legal consultations, financial coaching and eldercare referrals.

A family-forming benefit provides a lifetime maximum of USD 25,000 toward fertility treatment, surrogacy, adoption costs and related travel. It is available from the first day of medical eligibility, regardless of diagnosis, relationship status or sexual orientation.

Gender-affirming care is covered under every medical plan on the same terms as any other medically necessary care, with a dedicated care navigator available through the carrier.

A wellbeing allowance of USD 40 per month may be claimed for exercise, sports club membership, mindfulness applications or equipment. It is claimed in MosaicOne Expenses like any other allowance and is taxable where the entity's rules require it.

Backup care provides ten days per plan year of subsidised childcare or eldercare for the days when the usual arrangement falls through. The employee pays a fixed contribution per day and the company pays the rest.

None of these programmes reports individual usage to a manager, and none is visible on an employee's record beyond the payroll deduction where one applies.

How to Use Your Benefits

Three practical notes that answer most of the questions People Operations receives.

Find your card. Digital medical, dental and vision cards are in MosaicOne Benefits from the day coverage begins. Physical cards arrive within fifteen days. A provider who cannot find you is usually looking on the day before coverage started.

Check the network first. Every plan pays substantially more in network than out of it, and the carrier's directory is the authority, not the provider's own website. A five-minute check before an appointment is the single most effective thing an employee can do to control their own cost.

Appeal a denied claim. Carriers deny claims for administrative reasons more often than for clinical ones, and a first-level appeal succeeds surprisingly often. People Operations will help draft one, and will escalate on the employee's behalf where the carrier's own appeal has failed.

Employees leaving Mosaic Robotics keep medical, dental and vision cover to the end of the month in which they leave, and are sent continuation-coverage information within fourteen days of their final day.
