# PTO & Holidays Policy

Document ID: pto-and-holidays · Owner: People Operations · Effective 2026-01-01 · Version 2026.1
Topics: pto, holidays

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how paid time off (PTO) is earned, requested, approved and paid out at Mosaic Robotics, Inc. It applies to all employees of the
United States entity and, except where the *Germany Entity Differences* section of the Leave of Absence Policy says otherwise, to employees of Mosaic
Robotics GmbH. Contractors and interns are covered by the terms of their engagement letter and do not accrue PTO under this policy.

Mosaic Robotics runs on a calendar fiscal year. The PTO plan year therefore opens on 1 January and closes on 31 December, and every balance, cap and
carryover figure in this document is stated for a single plan year unless the text says otherwise. All PTO is recorded in MosaicOne, the company HRIS.
A balance that does not appear in MosaicOne does not exist for the purposes of this policy, and a manager cannot approve time off outside the system.

PTO at Mosaic Robotics is a single pooled bank covering vacation, personal time and short absences of convenience. Sick time is tracked separately and
is described near the end of this document. Statutory leave — parental, medical, family care, bereavement and civic leave — is governed by the Leave of
Absence Policy and never consumes PTO.

Three principles shape everything below. First, time off is a benefit the company expects people to use, not a reserve to hoard. Second, a team's
delivery commitments and a person's rest are both real, so the policy sets notice periods long enough for a manager to plan around an absence. Third,
every decision is recorded: the request, the approval, the balance at the time of approval and any exception are all written to MosaicOne so that a
question six months later has a documented answer.

## Accrual

### Standard Accrual Rates

PTO accrues monthly and is posted to the employee's MosaicOne balance on the first calendar day of each month. Accrual is not pro-rated within a month:
an employee who is active on the posting date receives the full monthly posting for that month.

Full-time employees with fewer than three years of service accrue 1.25 days of PTO per month. Full-time employees with three or more years of service
accrue 1.50 days of PTO per month. The service band is evaluated on the posting date using the employee's most recent hire date as recorded in
MosaicOne; a promotion, transfer or change of manager does not reset it, and neither does an internal move between the United States and Germany
entities.

At 1.25 days per month an employee earns 15.0 days across a full plan year. At 1.50 days per month the annual figure is 18.0 days. An employee who
crosses the three-year mark mid-year accrues at the lower rate for the months before the anniversary and at the higher rate from the first posting date
on or after it; there is no retroactive true-up for the months already posted.

New hires begin accruing on the first posting date on or after their start date. An employee who starts on 20 March therefore receives their first
posting on 1 April. There is no waiting period for *using* accrued PTO — an employee may request time off against a positive balance from day one — but
Mosaic Robotics does not permit a negative PTO balance, so a new hire cannot borrow against future accrual.

### Part-Time and Prorated Accrual

Part-time employees scheduled at 0.5 FTE or more accrue PTO in proportion to their FTE. A 0.6 FTE employee in the under-three-year band accrues 0.75
days per month, and the same employee in the three-year-plus band accrues 0.90 days per month. Proration is calculated to two decimal places and the
posted value is rounded to the nearest 0.05 days.

Employees scheduled below 0.5 FTE do not accrue PTO. They are instead paid for hours worked and may take unpaid time off with their manager's
agreement, recorded in MosaicOne as an unpaid absence so that payroll and headcount reporting stay accurate.

A change of FTE takes effect for accrual purposes on the first posting date on or after the effective date of the change. Balances already accrued at a
higher FTE are never clawed back.

### Accrual Caps

PTO accrual stops once an employee's balance reaches 30.0 days. The cap is a ceiling on the balance, not an annual limit on what may be earned: once
the employee uses enough PTO to bring the balance below 30.0 days, accrual resumes at the next posting date. Days that would have posted while the
balance sat at the cap are not recovered.

People Operations sends an automated reminder through MosaicOne when a balance reaches 26.0 days, and a manager notification at 29.0 days, so that a
person is not silently losing accrual. A manager who receives the 29.0-day notification is expected to work with the employee on a plan to take time
off within the following quarter.

## Requesting Time Off

### Notice Requirements

PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance. A request of six or more consecutive business days requires 15 business days of
advance notice. Notice is counted in business days between the day the request is submitted in MosaicOne and the first day of the requested absence,
excluding both the submission day and any company holiday on the employee's office calendar.

The notice requirement exists so that a manager can rebalance work, not to create a barrier. A manager may waive the notice requirement for a request
that falls inside the window; the waiver is recorded in MosaicOne with a short reason, and People Operations reviews waivers quarterly to make sure the
exception has not become the rule.

Unforeseeable absences — a sudden illness, a family emergency, a transport failure that makes work impossible — are never held to the notice
requirement. The employee notifies their manager as early as they reasonably can, and the absence is entered in MosaicOne retroactively, against sick
time where the reason was illness and against PTO otherwise.

### Approval Chain

Every PTO request requires written approval from the employee's direct manager in MosaicOne. Verbal agreement, a message in chat and an email thread
are all insufficient on their own: the approval must be recorded against the request so the audit trail is complete.

A request that has been open for three business days without a decision escalates automatically to the skip-level manager, as described in the Manager
Approval Matrix. Escalation does not approve the request; it moves the decision to someone who is available to make it.

Requests longer than 15 consecutive business days additionally require director approval. Requests that overlap a declared blackout period require
director approval regardless of length. In both cases MosaicOne routes the request to the second approver automatically once the direct manager has
approved it, and the employee sees both decisions on the request record.

### Blackout Periods

A blackout period is a short, published window during which the company restricts discretionary time off because a delivery or business event depends
on full staffing. Blackout periods are published in MosaicOne at least 60 calendar days before they begin, and no more than four business days of
blackout may be declared in any plan year.

The 2026 year-end production blackout runs from 22 December 2026 through 23 December 2026. It applies to the Manufacturing, Field Service and Customer
Support organisations. Employees outside those organisations may take PTO on those dates under the ordinary notice rules.

A request that overlaps a blackout period is not automatically refused. It is routed for director approval with the overlap flagged, and the director
weighs the business need against the employee's circumstances. An employee who booked non-refundable travel before the blackout was published is
expected to be accommodated.

## Carryover and Expiry

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5.0 days of unused PTO into the following plan year. Any balance above 5.0 days on 31 December is forfeited.
MosaicOne shows a projected forfeiture figure from 1 October so that nobody is surprised on New Year's Eve.

Carried-over PTO expires on 31 March of the following plan year and is forfeited if unused. Carryover is consumed first: a PTO day taken in February
draws down the carryover bucket before it touches the current year's accrual, which maximises the value the employee actually receives.

Two exceptions apply. An employee who was unable to take planned PTO because a request was refused for business reasons may ask People Operations to
extend their carryover expiry by up to 90 days; the request is made in MosaicOne and cites the refused request. An employee returning from a statutory
leave that spanned the year-end has their carryover expiry set to 90 days after their return-to-work date.

Germany entity employees are subject to statutory minimum vacation rules that in some cases require a longer carryover window than this policy grants.
Where the statute is more generous, the statute applies; People Operations administers the difference and the employee does not need to request it.

## Company Holidays

### United States Holiday Calendar

Mosaic Robotics observes 11 paid company holidays in the United States in 2026. The 2026 United States calendar is: New Year's Day (1 January), Martin
Luther King Jr. Day (19 January), Presidents' Day (16 February), Memorial Day (25 May), Juneteenth (19 June), Independence Day (observed 3 July),
Labor Day (7 September), Thanksgiving Day (26 November), the Day after Thanksgiving (27 November), Christmas Eve (24 December) and Christmas Day (25
December).

A holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed on the preceding Friday; a holiday that falls on a Sunday is observed on the following Monday. The
observed date, not the nominal date, is the paid day. Employees required to work on a company holiday because of an on-call rotation receive an
alternative paid day off, scheduled within 60 calendar days.

### Germany Holiday Calendar

The Berlin office observes 10 paid public holidays in 2026, following the Berlin state calendar. Because German public holidays are set by statute
rather than by the company, the Berlin calendar and the United States calendar do not match, and neither entity's employees may claim the other's
holidays.

Employees of the German entity who work with United States colleagues are not expected to be available on a Berlin public holiday, and United States
employees are not expected to be available on a United States company holiday. Meetings that span both calendars are scheduled around whichever
calendar has a holiday.

### Floating Holidays

Each employee receives 2 floating holidays per plan year. Floating holidays are intended for observances the company calendar does not cover — a
religious holiday, a national day, a family tradition — and may be taken for any reason.

Floating holidays are posted on 1 January, or on the start date for a new hire, and expire on 31 December. They do not carry over and are not paid out
at separation. A floating holiday is requested in MosaicOne exactly like a PTO day and is subject to the same 5-business-day notice requirement, but it
does not draw down the PTO balance.

## Unused PTO at Separation

Accrued and unused PTO is paid out at separation for employees on the US entity, up to the 30.0-day accrual cap. The payout is calculated at the
employee's base rate of pay on their final day and is included in the final paycheque.

Carried-over days that have not yet expired are included in the payout. Floating holidays, sick days and any unpaid absence balance are not. An
employee who separates with a negative balance — which can only occur where a manager waived the no-borrowing rule in writing — has the shortfall
deducted from the final paycheque where local law permits.

Germany entity separations follow German statutory vacation-payout rules, administered by People Operations with the local payroll provider. The
statutory calculation replaces this section entirely for those employees.

## Sick Time and Personal Days

Full-time employees receive 8 paid sick days per plan year, tracked separately from PTO. Sick days are posted in full on 1 January or on the start date
for a new hire, do not accrue monthly, do not carry over and are not paid out at separation.

Sick time may be used for the employee's own illness, a medical or dental appointment, or the care of an immediate family member who is ill. No
doctor's note is required for an absence of three consecutive days or fewer. An absence of four or more consecutive days for medical reasons should be
converted to a medical leave under the Leave of Absence Policy, which protects the employee's pay and benefits far better than sick time does.

Part-time employees scheduled at 0.5 FTE or more receive sick days in proportion to their FTE, rounded up to the nearest half day. An employee who
exhausts their sick balance and is still unwell uses PTO, and People Operations will help arrange a medical leave if the absence is likely to continue.

## Managing Time Off as a Manager

Managers are the first and usually the only approver in this policy, and the company asks four things of
them. Answer within three business days, so that an employee can book travel. Say yes unless there is a
concrete business reason to say no, and write the reason down when there is. Never ask why an employee
wants the time; the reason is not a factor in this policy and asking makes people uncomfortable enough
that they stop asking for time at all. And take your own time off, because a team reads its manager's
calendar far more carefully than it reads a policy document.

Coverage is the manager's problem to solve, not the employee's. Where two people on a small team ask for
the same week, the manager works out the split rather than asking them to negotiate between themselves.
Where a delivery genuinely cannot survive an absence, the honest conversation is about the delivery date,
not about the holiday.

MosaicOne gives every manager a team calendar showing approved and pending time off for the next six
months, and a quarterly summary of how much PTO each person has taken. The summary exists to catch the
opposite problem from the one managers expect: the person who has taken four days all year and is heading
for a forfeiture in December.

A manager who is going to be away for more than three business days delegates their approval authority in
MosaicOne, exactly as the Manager Approval Matrix requires. An undelegated absence turns every pending
request into an escalation to the skip-level manager, which is a worse outcome for everyone than thirty
seconds of setup.

Managers do not approve their own time off. A manager's request routes to their own manager, and a
department head's routes to the executive they report to.

## Questions

PTO questions go to People Operations at people-ops@mosaicrobotics.example. Balance discrepancies should be raised through MosaicOne's *Report a
balance issue* form, which attaches the current ledger to the case automatically and shortens the investigation considerably.

Managers who need help interpreting the notice or approval rules for an unusual request — a sabbatical-length absence, a request that straddles a
transfer between entities, a blackout overlap with non-refundable travel — should contact People Operations before answering the employee, so that the
first answer the employee receives is the right one.
